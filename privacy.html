Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

Lite Radio is a free app by nyasatech1 for listening to Malawian internet radio. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect your name, phone number, email or location, and we do not run a server that stores information about you.

What is saved on your phone

  • Your favourite stations.
  • A saved copy of the station list, so it can show up quickly and when your connection is poor.
  • The app’s own files, so it can open without internet.

This stays on your device. You can remove it by clearing the site data for Lite Radio in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Other services the app connects to

To work, the app contacts services run by other people. As with any website, they can see your IP address and basic device and browser details. We do not control them, and their own privacy policies apply.

  • Radio Browser (api.radio-browser.info): provides the list of stations. When you play a station, the app also tells Radio Browser which station was played, so it can count how popular stations are. It is not linked to a name or account.
  • Radio stations: audio streams come straight from each station’s own servers. The station can see that your device connected.
  • Station logos: logos are loaded from the image address the directory lists, or from the station website’s icon through Google’s favicon service. Google receives the website address being looked up and your IP address.
  • Google Fonts: used for the lettering of the “Lite Radio” title. Google receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Hosting: the company that hosts the app may keep standard server logs.

Cookies

Lite Radio does not set cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage and cache, as described above.

Children

Lite Radio is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can stop a station at any time, which also stops its data use. You can clear your favourites and saved data in your browser settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and its date.
